WHY sources (all index, search 2026-11-06):
- Invert-multiply: https://www.themathdoctors.org/dividing-fractions-why-invert-and-multiply/ and https://www.themathdoctors.org/dividing-fractions-how-and-why/ — multiply top+bottom by reciprocal makes divisor 1; also "how many divisor-sized pieces fit" (2 ÷ 1/5 = 10 because 5 fifths per whole). Also https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1096703.pdf and https://www.amesa.org.za/LTM 16_1.pdf (tip-and-times via ×1/equivalence).
- Regrouping: https://blog.bhanzu.com/parents/what-is-regrouping-in-math — 10 ones = 1 ten, 52 renamed 4 tens+12 ones, "borrow" is misleading, nothing repaid; https://mathify.ink/blog/how-to-explain-addition-subtraction-to-third-graders
- Area: https://www.mathteacherzhang.com/lessons/003-what-is-area/ — area = count unit squares; rectangle rows×columns; parallelogram cut-and-slide to rectangle; triangle = half parallelogram; trapezoid = two triangles / average of bases. Also https://tallybench.com/area-calculator.html
- Negative×negative: https://math.tools/blog/why-is-a-negative-times-a-negative-positive — pattern +4 each step, distributive law forces (-1)(-1)=1, flip-twice; https://seewhy.bishoy.io/lessons/004-why-does-a-negative-times-a-negative-equal-a-positive.pdf — flip once/twice, pattern -9,-6,-3,0.
- Cross-multiplication: https://math.tools/blog/why-does-cross-multiplication-work — multiply both sides by bd, denominators cancel; NOT for adding fractions.
- Equivalent fractions: https://www.meaningfulmathco.com/mm_math_terms/equivalent-fractions/ and https://thirdspacelearning.com/us/blog/what-are-equivalent-fractions/ — ×n/n = ×1, slicing each part smaller, shaded amount unchanged; adding same number to top+bottom is wrong.
- Distributive/area model: https://coprimes.com/math/distributive-property-multiplication/ — 4×(5+3)=4×5+4×3 as split rectangle; 6×48=6×40+6×8.
- Decimals ×/÷10: https://www.open.edu/openlearn/pluginfile.php/1523721/mod_oucontent/oucontent/86778/d2dc1c78/b2d03d34/wk1_vid08new_1_transcript.html — digits move, point does not; ×10 makes each digit 10× bigger.
- Misconceptions decimals: https://extranet.education.unimelb.edu.au/SME/TNMY/Decimals/Decimals/tests/miscon.htm — longer-is-larger ~40% Y5; https://deled.institute/teaching-mathematics/students-struggle-with-decimal-fractions/
- CPA/bar model: https://bhanzu.com/parents/what-is-singapore-math ; https://geniebook.com/exam-preparation/psle/article/bar-model-singapore-math-psle-guide
- Volume: https://classmathematics.com.au/resources/wa/year-9/maths/measurement/volume-of-prisms/ — V = cross-section area × length; https://revisiontown.com/squared-cubic-units-free-learning-resources/
- 2022 standards: https://www.pep.com.cn/bks/kcjcjf/xszh/202503/W020250410540192903150.pdf — 7 themes: 数与运算, 数量关系, 图形的认识与测量, 图形的位置与运动, 数据分类, 数据的收集整理与表达, 随机现象发生的可能性. Stages in cn-math-taxonomy standards-mapping: 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-9.
